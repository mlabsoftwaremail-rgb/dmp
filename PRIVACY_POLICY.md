# Privacy Policy for dmp

**Effective Date: August 16, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how the **dmp** ("we", "our", or "the App") handles your information. The App is a music and media player designed to provide a high-quality playback experience for local audio files and internet radio streams.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Local Data
The App requires access to your device's storage (`READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` or `READ_MEDIA_AUDIO`) to function. This access is used solely to:
*   Identify and list audio files stored on your device.
*   Read metadata (titles, artists, albums) for display within the App.
*   Play the audio files you select.

We **do not** upload your music files or their metadata to any external servers. All processing of local files happens entirely on your device.

### 1.2 User Preferences
We store your application settings (such as shuffle mode, favorites, and recently played lists) locally on your device using `SharedPreferences`. This data is not shared with us or any third parties.

### 1.3 Internet Radio Streams
When you use the internet radio feature, the App connects to the stream URLs you provide or select. This involves standard internet communication where your IP address may be visible to the stream provider, but the App does not collect or store this information.

## 2. Third-Party Services and Advertising

### 2.1 Advertising
Currently, the App does not display advertisements. However, we may integrate advertising services (such as Google AdMob) in the future to support development. These third-party services may collect information used to identify your device for the purpose of serving relevant ads.

When advertising is integrated, these third parties may collect:
*   Device identifiers (Android Advertising ID).
*   Approximate location data (IP-based).
*   Interaction data with advertisements.

### 2.2 Google Play Services
The App uses Google Play Services for core functionality and potential future analytics. Google’s privacy policy can be found at: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 3. Permissions Usage
*   **Notifications**: Used to display playback controls in the notification area.
*   **Foreground Service**: Required to keep the music playing while the App is in the background.
*   **Bluetooth**: Used to support playback through Bluetooth headsets and speakers.
*   **Internet**: Used for streaming radio stations.

## 4. Data Security
We value your trust in providing us your Personal Information, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. But remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

## 5. Children’s Privacy
These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
