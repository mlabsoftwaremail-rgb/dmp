# Privacy Policy for Digital Music Player (dmp)

**Last Updated: August 16, 2026**

**mlab.software** ("we," "us," or "our") operates the **Digital Music Player (dmp)** mobile application (Package Name: `com.mlab.software.dmp`). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our app and the choices you have associated with that data.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

### 1.1 Local Audio Data and Storage
Digital Music Player is primarily an offline media player. To function correctly, the app requires access to your device's storage:
*   **Permissions Used**: `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` (for Android 12 and below) and `READ_MEDIA_AUDIO` (for Android 13 and above).
*   **Usage**: These permissions are used solely to scan, list, and play audio files stored on your device.
*   **Data Handling**: We do not upload your music files or their metadata (titles, artists, albums) to any external servers. All processing of local files happens entirely on your device.

### 1.2 User Preferences and App Data
We store your application settings (such as volume levels, favorites, and recently played lists) locally on your device. This data is not shared with us or any third parties.

### 1.3 Internet Services
*   **Radio Streams**: If you use the internet radio feature, the app connects to the stream URLs provided. Standard internet communication (including your IP address) occurs between your device and the stream provider.
*   **Network State**: The app uses `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` and `INTERNET` permissions to manage stream playback and detect connection changes.

## 2. Third-Party Advertising (Future Integration)

Currently, the App does not display advertisements. However, to support ongoing development, we may integrate advertising services (such as **Google AdMob**) in the future.

When advertising is integrated, third-party partners may collect information to serve relevant ads:
*   **Device Identifiers**: Such as Android Advertising ID (AAID).
*   **Analytics**: Approximate location (IP-based) and ad interaction data.
*   **Cookies/Local Storage**: Ad partners may use cookies or local storage to facilitate ad delivery and personalization.

We will comply with the Google Play Store's policies regarding ad disclosures and user consent (including GDPR and CCPA) at the time of integration.

## 3. Third-Party Services
The App uses Google Play Services for core system functionality. You can find Google’s privacy policy here: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 4. Permissions Disclosure
*   **Notifications**: To display playback controls in the notification shade.
*   **Foreground Service**: To allow music playback to continue while the app is in the background.
*   **Bluetooth**: To support playback through Bluetooth devices.

## 5. Data Security and Encryption
We value your privacy. All data handled by the app is encrypted in transit using industry-standard protocols (e.g., HTTPS/TLS) when connecting to external streams.

## 6. Children’s Privacy
Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
